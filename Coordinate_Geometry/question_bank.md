# Coordinate Geometry: 50 Important Questions with Solutions (Class 10)

1. **Question:** Find distance between \((0,0)\) and \((3,4)\).
   **Solution:** \(\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\).

2. **Question:** Find distance between \((-2,5)\) and \((4,1)\).
   **Solution:** \(\sqrt{(6)^2+(-4)^2}=\sqrt{52}=2\sqrt{13}\).

3. **Question:** Find midpoint of \((2,3)\) and \((8,11)\).
   **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{3+11}{2}\right)=(5,7)\).

4. **Question:** Find midpoint of \((-4,7)\) and \((6,-3)\).
   **Solution:** \(\left(1,2\right)\).

5. **Question:** Find section point dividing \((1,2)\), \((7,8)\) in ratio \(1:2\) internally.
   **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{1\cdot7+2\cdot1}{3},\frac{1\cdot8+2\cdot2}{3}\right)=(3,4)\).

6. **Question:** Find section point dividing \((2,-1)\), \((8,5)\) in ratio \(2:1\) internally.
   **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{2\cdot8+1\cdot2}{3},\frac{2\cdot5+1(-1)}{3}\right)=(6,3)\).

7. **Question:** Find coordinates of point dividing \((3,4)\), \((9,10)\) in ratio \(1:1\).
   **Solution:** Midpoint \((6,7)\).

8. **Question:** Find \(k\) if distance between \((2,k)\) and \((2,5)\) is 7.
   **Solution:** \(|k-5|=7\Rightarrow k=12\) or \(-2\).

9. **Question:** Find \(x\) if distance between \((x,2)\) and \((4,2)\) is 9.
   **Solution:** \(|x-4|=9\Rightarrow x=13\) or \(-5\).

10. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((0,0),(4,0),(0,3)\).
    **Solution:** Right triangle area \(=\frac12\cdot4\cdot3=6\).

11. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((1,1),(4,1),(1,5)\).
    **Solution:** Base \(=3\), height \(=4\). Area \(=6\).

12. **Question:** Check if points \((1,2),(2,4),(3,6)\) are collinear.
    **Solution:** Slope AB=2, BC=2. Collinear.

13. **Question:** Check if points \((0,1),(1,3),(2,8)\) are collinear.
    **Solution:** Slope AB=2, BC=5. Not collinear.

14. **Question:** Find point on x-axis equidistant from \((2,3)\) and \((4,5)\).
    **Solution:** Let \((x,0)\). Equate distances: \((x-2)^2+9=(x-4)^2+25\Rightarrow x=7\). Point \((7,0)\).

15. **Question:** Find point on y-axis equidistant from \((3,2)\) and \((5,4)\).
    **Solution:** Let \((0,y)\). \(9+(y-2)^2=25+(y-4)^2\Rightarrow y=8\). Point \((0,8)\).

16. **Question:** Find ratio in which \((3,5)\) divides segment joining \((1,2)\), \((7,11)\).
    **Solution:** Let ratio \(m:n\). From x: \(\frac{7m+n}{m+n}=3\Rightarrow2m=n\). So ratio \(1:2\).

17. **Question:** Find ratio in which \((2,2)\) divides \((0,0)\) to \((5,5)\).
    **Solution:** Since coordinates are \(2/5\) of endpoint, ratio \(2:3\).

18. **Question:** Find point dividing \((0,0)\), \((6,9)\) in ratio \(2:1\).
    **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{2\cdot6}{3},\frac{2\cdot9}{3}\right)=(4,6)\).

19. **Question:** Distance between \((a,0)\), \((0,b)\) is 10 and \(a=6\). Find \(b\).
    **Solution:** \(\sqrt{36+b^2}=10\Rightarrow b=8\) or \(-8\).

20. **Question:** Find coordinates of centroid of triangle \((0,0),(6,0),(0,9)\).
    **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{6}{3},\frac{9}{3}\right)=(2,3)\).

21. **Question:** Find perimeter of triangle with vertices \((0,0),(3,0),(3,4)\).
    **Solution:** Sides \(3,4,5\). Perimeter \(12\).

22. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((2,3),(5,7),(9,11)\).
    **Solution:** Using determinant gives area \(0\). Points collinear.

23. **Question:** Find distance of point \((3,4)\) from origin.
    **Solution:** \(\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=5\).

24. **Question:** Find coordinates of point that divides \((2,6)\), \((8,0)\) in ratio \(3:1\).
    **Solution:** Using section formula with \(A(2,6),B(8,0),m:n=3:1\): \(P\left(\frac{m x_2+n x_1}{m+n},\frac{m y_2+n y_1}{m+n}\right)=\left(\frac{3\cdot8+1\cdot2}{4},\frac{3\cdot0+1\cdot6}{4}\right)=\left(\frac{13}{2},\frac{3}{2}\right)\).

25. **Question:** If midpoint of \((x,2)\), \((6,8)\) is \((4,5)\), find \(x\).
    **Solution:** \(\frac{x+6}{2}=4\Rightarrow x=2\).

26. **Question:** If midpoint of \((3,y)\), \((7,9)\) is \((5,6)\), find \(y\).
    **Solution:** \(\frac{y+9}{2}=6\Rightarrow y=3\).

27. **Question:** Find area of quadrilateral with vertices \((0,0),(4,0),(4,3),(0,3)\).
    **Solution:** Rectangle area \(=12\).

28. **Question:** Find point on line segment joining \((1,1)\), \((5,9)\) at 3/4th from first point.
    **Solution:** Ratio \(3:1\). Point \(\left(\frac{3\cdot5+1}{4},\frac{3\cdot9+1}{4}\right)=(4,7)\).

29. **Question:** Find whether triangle with sides from points \((0,0),(2,2),(4,0)\) is isosceles.
    **Solution:** Distances: \(\sqrt8,\sqrt8,4\). Two equal sides, so isosceles.

30. **Question:** Find value of \(k\) so that \((k,2)\), \((4,2)\), \((7,2)\) are collinear.
    **Solution:** Same y-coordinate, all collinear for any \(k\).

31. **Question:** Find \(k\) so that distance between \((k,0)\), \((0,0)\) is 6.
    **Solution:** \(|k|=6\Rightarrow k=\pm6\).

32. **Question:** Find area of triangle \((1,2),(3,5),(6,1)\).
    **Solution:** \(\frac12|1(5-1)+3(1-2)+6(2-5)|=\frac12|4-3-18|=\frac{17}{2}\).

33. **Question:** Verify if \((2,3)\) is midpoint of \((1,1)\), \((3,5)\).
    **Solution:** Midpoint \(=\left(2,3\right)\). Yes.

34. **Question:** Find point dividing \((4,-2)\), \((10,4)\) in ratio \(1:2\).
    **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{1\cdot10+2\cdot4}{3},\frac{1\cdot4+2(-2)}{3}\right)=(6,0)\).

35. **Question:** Find distance between \((-3,-4)\), \((0,0)\).
    **Solution:** \(\sqrt{9+16}=5\).

36. **Question:** Find midpoint of \((-5,-7)\), \((1,3)\).
    **Solution:** \((-2,-2)\).

37. **Question:** Determine if \((1,1),(2,3),(3,5)\) are collinear.
    **Solution:** Slopes \(2\) and \(2\). Collinear.

38. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((0,0),(a,0),(0,b)\).
    **Solution:** \(\frac12|ab|\).

39. **Question:** Find point on y-axis at distance 5 from \((3,4)\).
    **Solution:** Let \((0,y)\): \(9+(y-4)^2=25\Rightarrow(y-4)^2=16\Rightarrow y=8,0\). Points \((0,8),(0,0)\).

40. **Question:** Find point on x-axis at distance 13 from \((5,12)\).
    **Solution:** Let \((x,0)\): \((x-5)^2+144=169\Rightarrow(x-5)^2=25\Rightarrow x=10,0\). Points \((10,0),(0,0)\).

41. **Question:** Find ratio in which \((4,4)\) divides \((1,1)\), \((7,7)\).
    **Solution:** Midpoint of \((1,1)\) and \((7,7)\) is \(\left(\frac{1+7}{2},\frac{1+7}{2}\right)=(4,4)\). So it divides the segment in ratio \(1:1\).

42. **Question:** Find value of \(k\) if \((k,3)\) is midpoint of \((2,1)\), \((8,5)\).
    **Solution:** Midpoint x-coordinate \(=5\). So \(k=5\).

43. **Question:** Find point dividing \((-2,4)\), \((6,-8)\) in ratio \(3:1\).
    **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{3\cdot6+1(-2)}{4},\frac{3(-8)+1\cdot4}{4}\right)=(4,-5)\).

44. **Question:** A point P is equidistant from \((2,0)\) and \((0,2)\), and lies on \(y=x\). Find P.
    **Solution:** Every point on perpendicular bisector \(y=x\) is equidistant; infinitely many such points.

45. **Question:** Find whether triangle with vertices \((0,0),(1,1),(2,2)\) has area 0.
    **Solution:** Points collinear \(\Rightarrow\) area \(0\).

46. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((2,0),(0,2),(-2,0)\).
    **Solution:** Base between \((2,0),(-2,0)\)=4, height=2. Area \(=4\).

47. **Question:** Find distance between points where line \(y=2\) meets x=1 and x=6.
    **Solution:** Points \((1,2),(6,2)\). Distance \(5\).

48. **Question:** Midpoint of \((a,b)\), \((c,d)\) is \((0,0)\). Relation?
    **Solution:** \(a+c=0\), \(b+d=0\Rightarrow (c,d)=(-a,-b)\).

49. **Question:** Find point that divides \((0,0),(10,0)\) in ratio \(3:2\).
    **Solution:** \(\left(\frac{3\cdot10}{5},0\right)=(6,0)\).

50. **Question:** Find area of triangle with vertices \((1,1),(5,1),(3,4)\).
    **Solution:** Base \(=4\), height \(=3\). Area \(=\frac12\cdot4\cdot3=6\).
