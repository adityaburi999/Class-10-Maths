# Polynomials: 50 Important Questions with Solutions (Class 10)

1. **Question:** Find the zeroes of p(x)=x^2-5x+6.


   **Solution:** p(x)=(x-2)(x-3). Zeroes are 2,3.

2. **Question:** Find the zeroes of p(x)=2x^2+7x+3.


   **Solution:** 2x^2+7x+3=(2x+1)(x+3). Zeroes are -(1/2),-3.

3. **Question:** If one zero of x^2-7x+k is 3, find k.


   **Solution:** Put x=3: 9-21+k=0, so k=12.

4. **Question:** If zeroes of x^2-ax+20 are 4 and 5, find a.


   **Solution:** Sum of zeroes =a=4+5=9.

5. **Question:** Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes are -2 and 7.


   **Solution:** (x+2)(x-7)=x^2-5x-14.

6. **Question:** Verify relation between zeroes and coefficients for 3x^2-11x+6.


   **Solution:** Zeroes =3,(2/3). Sum =(11/3)=-(b/a), product =2=(c/a). Verified.

7. **Question:** Find remainder when 2x^3-3x^2+4x-5 is divided by x-2.


   **Solution:** Remainder =p(2)=16-12+8-5=7.

8. **Question:** Find remainder when x^3+2x^2-5x+1 is divided by x+1.


   **Solution:** Remainder =p(-1)=-1+2+5+1=7.

9. **Question:** Check whether x-1 is a factor of x^3-4x^2+x+6.


   **Solution:** p(1)=1-4+1+6=4≠0. Not a factor.

10. **Question:** Check whether x+2 is a factor of 2x^3+x^2-8x-4.


    **Solution:** p(-2)=-16+4+16-4=0. Yes, factor.

11. **Question:** Factorise x^3-6x^2+11x-6.


    **Solution:** Try roots 1,2,3. So (x-1)(x-2)(x-3).

12. **Question:** Factorise 2x^3-5x^2-4x+3.


    **Solution:** Root x=3 works. Divide to get 2x^2+x-1=(2x-1)(x+1). Final: (x-3)(2x-1)(x+1).

13. **Question:** Find zeroes of x^3-4x.


    **Solution:** x(x^2-4)=x(x-2)(x+2). Zeroes: -2,0,2.

14. **Question:** Find all zeroes of x^3+3x^2-4x-12.


    **Solution:** Grouping: x^2(x+3)-4(x+3)=(x+3)(x^2-4). Zeroes: -3,-2,2.

15. **Question:** Find a polynomial of degree 2 with zeroes (1/2),-3 and integer coefficients.


    **Solution:** (2x-1)(x+3)=2x^2+5x-3.

16. **Question:** If alpha,beta are zeroes of x^2-6x+8, find alpha^2+beta^2.


    **Solution:** alpha+beta=6,alphabeta=8. alpha^2+beta^2=36-16=20.

17. **Question:** For 2x^2-9x+7, find (1/alpha)+(1/beta).


    **Solution:** alpha+beta=(9/2),alphabeta=(7/2). Value =((alpha+beta)/(alphabeta))=(9/7).

18. **Question:** If one zero of x^2+px+16 is 4, find p.


    **Solution:** Other zero =4 (product 16). Sum =8=-p, so p=-8.

19. **Question:** Divide 2x^3+3x^2-11x-6 by x+3.


    **Solution:** Synthetic division gives quotient 2x^2-3x-2, remainder 0.

20. **Question:** Find k so that x-2 is a factor of x^3+kx^2-3x-2k.


    **Solution:** p(2)=8+4k-6-2k=2+2k=0, so k=-1.

21. **Question:** Find a cubic polynomial with zeroes 1,-1,2.


    **Solution:** (x-1)(x+1)(x-2)=x^3-2x^2-x+2.

22. **Question:** If zeroes of x^2-5x+m are equal, find m.


    **Solution:** Discriminant 25-4m=0, so m=(25/4).

23. **Question:** For p(x)=x^2-4x+3, find p(0),p(1),p(3).


    **Solution:** p(0)=3, p(1)=0, p(3)=0.

24. **Question:** Find the remainder when x^4-1 is divided by x-1.


    **Solution:** p(1)=0. Remainder 0.

25. **Question:** Find the remainder when x^4+2x^3-x+5 is divided by x+2.


    **Solution:** p(-2)=16-16+2+5=7.

26. **Question:** Find polynomial with zeroes 3 and -(1/3).


    **Solution:** (x-3)(3x+1)=3x^2-8x-3.

27. **Question:** If sum and product of zeroes are 7 and 10, form polynomial.


    **Solution:** x^2-7x+10.

28. **Question:** If alpha,beta are zeroes of 2x^2+5x-3, find alphabeta(alpha+beta).


    **Solution:** alpha+beta=-(5/2),alphabeta=-(3/2). Product =(15/4).

29. **Question:** Factorise x^3+6x^2+11x+6.


    **Solution:** (x+1)(x+2)(x+3).

30. **Question:** Find all zeroes of x(x-5)(x+2).


    **Solution:** x=0,5,-2.

31. **Question:** If one zero of 2x^2-7x+3 is 3, find the other.


    **Solution:** Product =(3/2). Other zero =(1/2).

32. **Question:** Determine k so that x+1 is factor of x^3+kx^2+x+1.


    **Solution:** p(-1)=-1+k-1+1=k-1=0, so k=1.

33. **Question:** Find quotient when x^3-1 is divided by x-1.


    **Solution:** Quotient x^2+x+1, remainder 0.

34. **Question:** Find quotient and remainder of x^3+4x^2+5x+2 by x+1.


    **Solution:** Quotient x^2+3x+2, remainder 0.

35. **Question:** Write x^3-8 as product of linear and quadratic factors.


    **Solution:** (x-2)(x^2+2x+4).

36. **Question:** Write 8x^3+1 as product of factors.


    **Solution:** (2x+1)(4x^2-2x+1).

37. **Question:** If alpha,beta are zeroes of x^2-2x-8, find alpha^3+beta^3.


    **Solution:** alpha+beta=2,alphabeta=-8. alpha^3+beta^3=(alpha+beta)^3-3alphabeta(alpha+beta)=8+48=56.

38. **Question:** Find polynomial with zeroes 2+sqrt(3) and 2-sqrt(3).


    **Solution:** Sum =4, product =1. Polynomial x^2-4x+1.

39. **Question:** If alpha,beta are zeroes of x^2+4x+1, find alpha^2beta^2.


    **Solution:** (alphabeta)^2=1.

40. **Question:** Find k if x-3 divides x^3-4x^2+kx+6.


    **Solution:** p(3)=27-36+3k+6=3k-3=0, so k=1.

41. **Question:** Check if x^2+1 can have real zeroes.


    **Solution:** x^2=-1 impossible in reals. No real zero.

42. **Question:** Find the number of zeroes of p(x)=x^2 from graph idea.


    **Solution:** Graph touches x-axis at x=0. One distinct real zero.

43. **Question:** If p(x)=x^2-9, find points where graph cuts x-axis.


    **Solution:** x^2-9=0, so x=±3. Points (-3,0),(3,0).

44. **Question:** Find remainder when x^5-3x+2 is divided by x.


    **Solution:** Remainder =p(0)=2.

45. **Question:** Find remainder when x^5-3x+2 is divided by x-1.


    **Solution:** p(1)=1-3+2=0.

46. **Question:** If x^2-(m+1)x+m has zeroes 1 and m, verify.


    **Solution:** (x-1)(x-m)=x^2-(m+1)x+m. Verified.

47. **Question:** Find m if one zero of x^2+mx+9 is 3.


    **Solution:** Other zero =3. Sum =6=-m, so m=-6.

48. **Question:** For p(x)=x^3-3x^2-4x+12, find all zeroes.


    **Solution:** Grouping: x^2(x-3)-4(x-3)=(x-3)(x^2-4). Zeroes: 3,2,-2.

49. **Question:** Find a quadratic polynomial whose zeroes differ by 2 and sum is 8.


    **Solution:** Zeroes 5,3. Polynomial x^2-8x+15.

50. **Question:** If alpha,beta are zeroes of 3x^2-5x-2, find alpha^2+beta^2.


    **Solution:** alpha+beta=(5/3),alphabeta=-(2/3). alpha^2+beta^2=(25/9)+(4/3)=(37/9).
