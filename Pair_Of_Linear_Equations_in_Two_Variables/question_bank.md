# Pair of Linear Equations in Two Variables: 50 Important Questions with Solutions (Class 10)

1. **Question:** Solve: \(x+y=7,\ x-y=1\).
   **Solution:** Add: \(2x=8\Rightarrow x=4\), then \(y=3\).

2. **Question:** Solve: \(2x+3y=13,\ 3x+2y=12\).
   **Solution:** Eliminate \(x\): \(6x+9y=39\), \(6x+4y=24\Rightarrow5y=15\Rightarrow y=3\), \(x=2\).

3. **Question:** Solve: \(5x-2y=1,\ 3x+4y=17\).
   **Solution:** Multiply first by 2: \(10x-4y=2\). Add with second×1? Better second×1 gives \(3x+4y=17\): \(13x=19\Rightarrow x=\frac{19}{13}\), \(y=\frac{41}{13}\).

4. **Question:** Solve by substitution: \(x=2y+1\), \(3x+y=19\).
   **Solution:** \(3(2y+1)+y=19\Rightarrow7y=16\Rightarrow y=\frac{16}{7}\), \(x=\frac{39}{7}\).

5. **Question:** Solve: \(4x+5y=6,\ 8x+10y=12\).
   **Solution:** Second is double of first. Infinitely many solutions.

6. **Question:** Solve: \(2x+3y=5,\ 4x+6y=15\).
   **Solution:** LHS ratio equal, constants not. No solution.

7. **Question:** Find \(k\) for unique solution: \(2x+ky=3,\ 4x+6y=5\).
   **Solution:** Unique if \(\frac{2}{4}\neq\frac{k}{6}\Rightarrow \frac12\neq\frac{k}{6}\Rightarrow k\neq3\).

8. **Question:** Find \(k\) for no solution: \(3x+2y=5,\ 9x+ky=12\).
   **Solution:** No solution when \(\frac{3}{9}=\frac{2}{k}\neq\frac{5}{12}\). So \(\frac13=\frac{2}{k}\Rightarrow k=6\).

9. **Question:** Find \(k\) for infinitely many solutions: \(2x+3y=7,\ 6x+ky=21\).
   **Solution:** Need \(\frac{2}{6}=\frac{3}{k}=\frac{7}{21}=\frac13\Rightarrow k=9\).

10. **Question:** Solve: \(7x+3y=29,\ 5x-2y=4\).
    **Solution:** Multiply first by 2, second by 3: \(14x+6y=58\), \(15x-6y=12\). Add: \(29x=70\Rightarrow x=\frac{70}{29}\), \(y=\frac{117}{29}\).

11. **Question:** Solve: \(x+2y=10,\ 3x-y=5\).
    **Solution:** From first \(x=10-2y\). Put in second: \(30-6y-y=5\Rightarrow y=\frac{25}{7}\), \(x=\frac{20}{7}\).

12. **Question:** Solve: \(0.2x+0.3y=1.3,\ 0.4x-0.5y=0.3\).
    **Solution:** Multiply by 10: \(2x+3y=13,\ 4x-5y=3\). Solve: \(x=\frac{37}{22},y=\frac{35}{11}\).

13. **Question:** Solve: \(\frac{x}{2}+\frac{y}{3}=4,\ \frac{x}{3}-\frac{y}{2}=1\).
    **Solution:** Multiply by 6: \(3x+2y=24,\ 2x-3y=6\). Solve: \(x=\frac{84}{13},y=\frac{30}{13}\).

14. **Question:** The sum of two numbers is 27 and difference is 5. Find numbers.
    **Solution:** Let \(x+y=27, x-y=5\). So \(x=16,y=11\).

15. **Question:** A father is 3 times as old as son. In 10 years, father will be twice son’s age. Find present ages.
    **Solution:** Let son \(=s\), father \(=3s\). \(3s+10=2(s+10)\Rightarrow s=10\), father \(=30\).

16. **Question:** Tickets: adult ₹50, child ₹30. Total 40 tickets, ₹1600 collected. Find numbers.
    **Solution:** \(a+c=40, 50a+30c=1600\Rightarrow5a+3c=160\). Solve: \(a=20,c=20\).

17. **Question:** Perimeter of rectangle is 54 cm and length is 3 cm more than breadth. Find dimensions.
    **Solution:** \(l+b=27, l-b=3\Rightarrow l=15,b=12\).

18. **Question:** 2 pens and 3 pencils cost ₹27; 4 pens and 1 pencil cost ₹31. Find costs.
    **Solution:** Let pen \(p\), pencil \(q\). Solve \(2p+3q=27,4p+q=31\Rightarrow p=6,q=5\).

19. **Question:** Solve graphically/intersection: \(x+y=6\), \(x+2y=8\).
    **Solution:** Subtract equations: \(y=2\), then \(x=4\). Intersection \((4,2)\).

20. **Question:** Solve: \(3x+4y=10\), \(6x+8y=20\).
    **Solution:** Dependent equations. Infinitely many solutions.

21. **Question:** Solve: \(x-3y=2\), \(2x-6y=5\).
    **Solution:** Parallel lines (inconsistent). No solution.

22. **Question:** Find \(k\) so that \(kx+3y=7\), \(9x+6y=14\) has no solution.
    **Solution:** No solution: \(\frac{k}{9}=\frac{3}{6}\neq\frac{7}{14}\). Since \(\frac{3}{6}=\frac{7}{14}=\frac12\), impossible. **No such \(k\)**.

23. **Question:** Solve: \(9x-2y=7\), \(3x+y=5\).
    **Solution:** From second \(y=5-3x\). Put in first: \(9x-2(5-3x)=7\Rightarrow15x=17\Rightarrow x=\frac{17}{15},y=\frac{8}{5}\).

24. **Question:** Find values if \(x:y=2:3\) and \(x+y=25\).
    **Solution:** Let \(x=2k,y=3k\). \(5k=25\Rightarrow k=5\Rightarrow x=10,y=15\).

25. **Question:** Solve: \(2x-y=3\), \(x+3y=11\).
    **Solution:** From first \(y=2x-3\). Put in second: \(x+6x-9=11\Rightarrow x=\frac{20}{7}, y=\frac{19}{7}\).

26. **Question:** Two-digit number has digits sum 9 and number is 27 more than reversed. Find number.
    **Solution:** Let tens \(x\), ones \(y\): \(x+y=9\), \(10x+y=10y+x+27\Rightarrow x-y=3\). So \(x=6,y=3\). Number \(63\).

27. **Question:** Solve: \(5x+7y=52\), \(3x-2y=1\).
    **Solution:** Eliminate \(y\): \(10x+14y=104\), \(21x-14y=7\). Add: \(31x=111\Rightarrow x=\frac{111}{31}\), \(y=\frac{151}{31}\).

28. **Question:** Solve: \(x+y=0\), \(2x+3y=5\).
    **Solution:** \(x=-y\). Then \(-2y+3y=5\Rightarrow y=5, x=-5\).

29. **Question:** Find \(k\) so that \(2x+3y=8\), \(4x+ky=16\) has infinitely many solutions.
    **Solution:** \(\frac{2}{4}=\frac{3}{k}=\frac{8}{16}=\frac12\Rightarrow k=6\).

30. **Question:** Solve by cross-multiplication: \(3x+2y=12\), \(5x-4y=4\).
    **Solution:** Solving gives \(x=\frac{28}{11}, y=\frac{24}{11}\).

31. **Question:** Sum of numerator and denominator is 10. If 3 is added to both, fraction becomes \(\frac{3}{4}\). Find fraction.
    **Solution:** Let numerator \(x\), denominator \(y\): \(x+y=10\), \(\frac{x+3}{y+3}=\frac34\Rightarrow4x-3y=-3\). Solve: \(x=3,y=7\). Fraction \(\frac37\).

32. **Question:** Solve: \(4x-5y=9\), \(6x+5y=21\).
    **Solution:** Add: \(10x=30\Rightarrow x=3\), then \(12-5y=9\Rightarrow y=\frac35\).

33. **Question:** Find points where lines \(x+2y=8\) and \(3x-y=5\) meet.
    **Solution:** From second \(y=3x-5\). Put in first: \(x+6x-10=8\Rightarrow x=\frac{18}{7}\), \(y=\frac{19}{7}\).

34. **Question:** Solve: \(2x+3y=11\), \(3x+2y=9\).
    **Solution:** Eliminate: \(6x+9y=33\), \(6x+4y=18\Rightarrow5y=15\Rightarrow y=3\), \(x=1\).

35. **Question:** Solve: \(7x-4y=10\), \(14x-8y=20\).
    **Solution:** Same equation. Infinitely many solutions.

36. **Question:** Solve: \(8x+3y=7\), \(16x+6y=20\).
    **Solution:** LHS ratios same, constants differ. No solution.

37. **Question:** Find \(k\) for unique solution: \(kx+2y=5\), \(6x+4y=7\).
    **Solution:** Unique if \(\frac{k}{6}\neq\frac{2}{4}=\frac12\Rightarrow k\neq3\).

38. **Question:** A boat goes 30 km downstream and 18 km upstream in 4 h. In still water it goes 12 km/h. Find stream speed.
    **Solution:** Let stream speed \(s\). Then \(\frac{30}{12+s}+\frac{18}{12-s}=4\Rightarrow s=3\) km/h.

39. **Question:** Solve: \(x-2y=-1\), \(3x+4y=19\).
    **Solution:** From first \(x=2y-1\). Put: \(6y-3+4y=19\Rightarrow10y=22\Rightarrow y=\frac{11}{5}, x=\frac{17}{5}\).

40. **Question:** Solve: \(11x+7y=5\), \(7x+11y=13\).
    **Solution:** Subtract: \(4x-4y=-8\Rightarrow x-y=-2\). Using first: \(11(y-2)+7y=5\Rightarrow18y=27\Rightarrow y=\frac32,x=-\frac12\).

41. **Question:** Solve: \(\frac{x}{5}+\frac{y}{2}=7,\ \frac{x}{3}-\frac{y}{4}=1\).
    **Solution:** Multiply by 10 and 12: \(2x+5y=70,\ 4x-3y=12\). Solve: \(x=\frac{246}{13},y=\frac{84}{13}\).

42. **Question:** If \(a+b=13\), \(a-b=1\), find \(a,b\).
    **Solution:** \(2a=14\Rightarrow a=7\), \(b=6\).

43. **Question:** Find two numbers whose sum is 41 and one is 9 less than twice the other.
    **Solution:** Let \(x+y=41\), \(x=2y-9\). Then \(3y=50\Rightarrow y=\frac{50}{3}, x=\frac{73}{3}\).

44. **Question:** Solve: \(2x+5y=3\), \(4x+10y=6\).
    **Solution:** Same equation. Infinitely many solutions.

45. **Question:** Solve: \(2x+5y=3\), \(4x+10y=9\).
    **Solution:** Inconsistent. No solution.

46. **Question:** Solve: \(3x-5y=7\), \(9x-15y=21\).
    **Solution:** Dependent equations. Infinitely many solutions.

47. **Question:** Find \(k\) such that system has no solution: \(2x+3y=4\), \(4x+ky=9\).
    **Solution:** Need \(\frac{2}{4}=\frac{3}{k}\neq\frac{4}{9}\). So \(k=6\).

48. **Question:** Solve: \(x+y=9\), \(2x-y=3\).
    **Solution:** Add: \(3x=12\Rightarrow x=4\), \(y=5\).

49. **Question:** Solve: \(5x+2y=19\), \(x-3y=-8\).
    **Solution:** From second \(x=3y-8\). Put: \(15y-40+2y=19\Rightarrow17y=59\Rightarrow y=\frac{59}{17}\), \(x=\frac{41}{17}\).

50. **Question:** Solve: \(3x+7y=20\), \(6x-7y=8\).
    **Solution:** Add: \(9x=28\Rightarrow x=\frac{28}{9}\). Then \(3x+7y=20\Rightarrow y=\frac{32}{21}\).
